PRIVACY POLICY

Markley Snow Services · Bucks & Montgomery County, PA

Information We Collect

When you call, text, or contact Markley Snow Services, we may receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, property details, and service needs.

Information Collected via Text or Call

When you call or text the number listed on this site, Markley Snow Services may receive your phone number, name, property address or location details, and the content of your message (e.g., service request, job inquiry). We do not collect this information through online forms, cookies, or account sign-ups, since the site has none.

How We Use It

Mike uses your information only to respond to your request, discuss snow-management services, coordinate work, or respond to a hiring inquiry. We do not sell your information.

Specifically, information you share is used to:

  • Respond to your service or hiring inquiry
  • Schedule or coordinate snow-management work
  • Maintain records for active or recurring commercial contracts
  • Follow up on subcontractor or crew opportunities

It is not used for unrelated marketing and is not sold.

SMS Consent & Opt-Out

If you text Mike, you consent to receive replies related to your inquiry, active service coordination, or hiring process. Consent to receive texts is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for support. Standard message/data rates may apply.

Information Sharing

Markley Snow Services does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information may be shared only where necessary to coordinate a job (e.g., with a subcontractor or crew member assigned to your property) or where required by law.

Data Retention

Contact and job information is retained only as long as needed to fulfill a service request, maintain an active contract, or comply with legal/recordkeeping obligations, after which it may be deleted.

Website Analytics

This site may use basic, aggregated website analytics (e.g., page visits) to understand general traffic. No personally identifying information is collected through analytics beyond what a visitor voluntarily provides by phone or text.

Children's Privacy

This site and its services are intended for property owners, managers, and prospective adult subcontractors/employees. It is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect information from, individuals under 18.

Your Choices

You may ask Mike directly, by phone or text, to review, correct, or delete any information he holds about you.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically by posting a revised version on this page.
